# Bike Tech archive

*Bike Tech: Bicycling Magazine's Newsletter for the Technical Enthusiast*,
published by Rodale Press, Emmaus, PA. A complete run: the 1982 pilot
issue plus all 5 volumes through the newsletter's last issue (Winter
1986) — 24 issues total. Scanned and hosted by
[bulgier.net](http://bulgier.net/pics/bike/Articles/Bike_Tech/) (scanning
credited there to Truls Erik Johnsen); mirrored here so the archive
survives independent of that one site staying up.

This is a full periodical archive, not a set of discrete academic
citations — kept separate from the individually-cited papers in `papers/`
and the rest of `literature/` for that reason. If an article here turns
out directly relevant to something in this engine or `MTB-wheel-lab`'s
regression, cite it properly in the relevant README rather than leaving
the reference only here (see "Directly wheel-relevant issues" below for
the ones already worth that treatment).

Titles/authors above were read directly off each issue's cover/table of
contents, not inferred from filenames — the filenames on the original
scans didn't reliably correspond to these (e.g. Vol. 1 numbered its files
`1_1`-`1_4`, Vol. 3 onward switched to `vol_3_1` etc.). Read from
moderate-resolution cover scans, not OCR'd or independently verified word
for word — a title in this table caught one transcription error already
(Vol. 3 No. 4's title was initially misread; fixed after re-checking at
higher resolution). Treat the index as a reliable *finding aid*, but
re-check the actual PDF before formally citing a title or author from
here. The "Lead article" column is also only the cover headline for most
issues — several issues carry other wheel-relevant pieces inside that
wouldn't show up from the cover alone (Vol. 3 No. 4 above is one example);
worth a full table-of-contents pass if this archive gets used for
anything beyond casual reading.

## Directly wheel-relevant issues

- **Vol. 4 No. 3 (June 1985) — "Stiffness Characteristics of Bicycle
  Wheels," Dan Price & Arthur Akers.** This is the paper cited widely in
  the wheel-mechanics literature (Gavin 1996 compares against it directly)
  that had no PDF anywhere else — now archived here. Cite as: Price, D.
  and Akers, A. (1985). "Stiffness Characteristics of Bicycle Wheels."
  *Bike Tech*, 4(3), Rodale Press, Emmaus, PA. Cross-referenced from the
  main `literature/README.md` citation table.
- **Vol. 3 No. 2 (April 1984) — "The Aluminum Rim: Design and Function,"
  Chris Juden.** Rim cross-section anatomy and design terminology
  (flange, bead seat, well, brake track, stagger) directly relevant to
  how `MTB-wheel-lab`'s rim catalogue and `rim_stiffness_index` estimate
  describe rim geometry.
- **Vol. 2 No. 5 (October 1983) — "Heat-Treated Rims — Are They Worth the
  Money?"** Uncredited/staff piece; worth a read if the regression ever
  adds a rim heat-treatment or alloy-temper beta.
- **Vol. 3 No. 4 (August 1984) — "A Musical Test for Correct Spoke
  Tension," John S. Allen** (Shop Talk section, not the cover article).
  An acoustic tension-check method predating Ford's 2018 acoustic
  rim-stiffness paper by over 30 years — worth reading alongside
  `papers/ford-peng-balogun-2018-acoustic-modal-testing.pdf` for how the
  same basic idea (pluck it, listen to the pitch) was already established
  practice for individual spoke tension long before it was formalized for
  whole-rim stiffness. Same issue's "Spoke Drag" (Glen Brown) looks at
  wheel choice's effect on aerodynamic drag.
